Welcome to RAPI AI ("we", "app", or "service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and transparently explaining how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

By using the RAPI AI application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

3. How We Use Your Data

3.1 Service Provision

  • Process your questions through AI (Google Gemini API)
  • Provide personalized recommendations based on your profile
  • Store conversation history for ongoing context

3.2 Service Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve response quality
  • Optimize application performance
  • Develop new features

3.3 Security

  • User authentication and authorization
  • Prevent service abuse
  • Rate limiting to protect the system

4. Data Sharing with Third Parties

4.1 Google Gemini API

Data shared:

  • Your chat messages
  • Profile context (role, company type, work culture)
  • Limited conversation history for context

Purpose:

Generate relevant and contextual AI responses

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Important Note:

  • We DO NOT share your email or password with Google Gemini
  • Data is sent via encrypted connection (HTTPS)
  • We have no control over how Google processes data after it's sent

4.2 Other Third Parties

We DO NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with other third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Storage Location

  • Database: PostgreSQL (account and profile data)
  • Redis: Temporary chat history
  • Server: Backend API on cloud infrastructure

5.2 Security

  • Passwords: Stored with hashing (bcrypt)
  • Communication: HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Authentication: JWT with expiry time
  • Access Control: Role-based authorization

5.3 Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data stored while account is active
  • Chat history: Stored in Redis with TTL (Time To Live)
  • After account deletion: Data permanently deleted within 30 days
