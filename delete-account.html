You can delete your RAPI AI account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1: Delete In-App (Recommended)

Mobile App Deletion
  1. Open the RAPI AI mobile app
  2. Navigate to Settings (from the menu)
  3. Scroll down to Account section
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm deletion when prompted
  6. Your account will be deleted immediately

Recommended: This is the fastest and most direct way to delete your account.

Option 2: Email Request

Email Deletion Request

If you cannot access the app, send an email to:

Email: fadlystwn@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include in your email:

  • Your registered email address
  • Confirmation that you want to delete your account
  • (Optional) Reason for deletion

We will process your request within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Account information (email, name, password)
  • Profile data (role, company type, work culture)
  • All chat messages and conversation history
  • Session data
  • Authentication tokens

Timeline: All data is permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion.

Important Notes

Warning: This Action is Permanent

  • Account deletion cannot be undone
  • You will need to create a new account to use RAPI AI again
  • All conversation history will be lost

Confirmation

  • You will receive an email confirmation after deletion
  • If you don't receive confirmation within 24 hours, contact us

Data Shared with Third Parties

  • Chat messages previously sent to Google Gemini API are subject to Google's data retention policies
  • We cannot delete data from Google's systems
  • See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Need Help?
